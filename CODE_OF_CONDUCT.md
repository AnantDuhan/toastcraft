# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want toastcraft to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. Maintainers and contributors pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and code review, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, email the maintainer at **duhananant@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential. You'll get a reply within a few days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may respond with a private warning, a temporary ban from the project's spaces or, for serious or repeated problems, a permanent ban. Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code may be edited or removed.
